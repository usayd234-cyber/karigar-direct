Connecting Local Skills with Local Homes

Karigar Direct is a community-first initiative built to digitize and support local skilled workers directly.

Who We Are & What We Do

Karigar Direct is a streamlined local directory designed to help households instantly locate verified skilled tradespeople—including electricians, plumbers, carpenters, and mechanics—within their immediate area. We operate without intermediaries or commission fees to ensure transparent local service.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower local skilled tradespeople by providing them with a distinct digital identity. Many talented workers rely solely on shop foot-traffic or word-of-mouth recommendations. We strive to offer every technician sustainable local work opportunity and dignity without middleman dependency.

Our Vision

We envision a transparent, 100% commission-free marketplace where local workers retain full earnings from their manual labor. Our goal is to establish accessible, community-driven service directories across every neighborhood to build direct trust and communication.

Our Commitment

⚡ Direct Contact

No customer service middleman. Call or WhatsApp technicians directly.

💰 Zero Commission

We charge 0% fees to both clients and workers. Fair work for full compensation.

🤝 Community First

Promoting local trade talents while solving everyday home repair needs efficiently.
